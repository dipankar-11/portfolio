PROFILE

About & Philosophy

I am Dipankar Das, a B.Sc. Computer Science student currently in my third semester at Bahona College, Assam. I am developing my technical foundation through web development, digital tools and hands-on creative practice.

Alongside my studies, I have created digital content, captions and basic designs for online platforms since 2023. Since 2025, I have been learning web development fundamentals and modern digital tools. I also enjoy video and photo editing and am developing my graphic design skills through logo and visual identity work. I am preparing my current logo concepts for publication and exploring freelance opportunities. I am at the start of my professional journey, so the work shown here includes ongoing practice as well as completed self-directed projects.

"Keep learning. Build with intent. Make every detail count."
